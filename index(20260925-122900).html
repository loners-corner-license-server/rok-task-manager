CONTROLS & NOTES

What to know while it runs.

Shortcuts

Ctrl + P Pause

Ctrl + Esc Stop and close the app

Restart behavior

After the app is closed and restarted, the functions will run through the characters and accounts again. It does not recall the previous app run.

Close the app once you are done with all of your accounts.

Usual bugs

  • May click the in-game shop
  • May click the RSS information in the top-right corner
  • May miss city RSS when it is behind the chat box

FAQ

Quick answers.

These answers reflect the supplied Task Manager notes.

Join Discord
Does it work with an emulator?

No. The supported setup is the ROK PC client only.

What display settings are required?

Use 1920 × 1080 display resolution, 100% Windows display scale, fullscreen, Ultra graphics, and Original map style.

Which characters are processed?

Automatic character switching uses starred characters.

Does it switch accounts?

Yes. After the characters are completed, it can switch to the next saved account login. The supplied notes state that the ROK PC version can retain 10 log-ins.

Does the app remember the previous run after I close it?

No. Restarting the app runs the functions through the characters and accounts again.
