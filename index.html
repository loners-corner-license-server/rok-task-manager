Does it work with emulators?

No. This build is designed for the official Windows PC client.

What display settings are required?

Use 1920×1080 resolution, 100% display scaling, fullscreen, Ultra graphics, and the original map style.

Can it work through multiple characters and accounts?

Yes. It can move through starred characters and saved account logins as part of the configured workflow.

Is there a free demo?

Yes. The free Windows demo includes Alliance Donation, Alliance Help, 1 Troop March, Gather Alliance Pit, Character Switching, and Google Account Switching. The full paid version adds the complete task set.

No. ROK Task Manager is an independent third-party tool by Loner's Corner and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's publisher or developer.
